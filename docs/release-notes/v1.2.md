# HBC+ v1.2 — ready for 1 January 2027

From 1 January 2027 Dutch net metering ends: an exported kWh earns less than an imported kWh costs. v1.2 lets HBC+
work with real buy **and** sell prices, and protects you against negative prices. With default settings it behaves
exactly like v1.1.

## What's new

- **Grid Clock feed** — choose *Grid Clock* as tariff source and pick your supplier (in Belgium also your grid
  operator). HBC+ downloads all-in buy and sell prices per quarter from the public files on `cdn.gridclock.eu`: no
  account, no token, no registration, and Grid Clock stores nothing about you.
- **Buy and sell prices** — every quarter has both. Dynamic values *Sell* at what an exported kWh really earns, so it
  sells less often once net metering ends. New *Sell now* tile and a sell line in the price chart.
- **Price guard** — while prices are negative it stops exporting and charges instead, and signals a PV limiter.
  Off by default; try it on *Monitor* first.
- **New dashboard layout** — nine views in the order you use them: Overview, Prices, Charge & Sell, Timed & Dynamic,
  Phase guard, PID Control, Batteries, Settings and Logging. The view addresses stay the same, so links keep working.
- **Logo** — HBC+ has a logo, in the dashboard header and at the top of the README.
- **Changed:** the tariff source *Grid Clock* reads the Grid Clock feed instead of the Grid Clock integration, which is
  no longer needed.

## Updating from v1.1

1. Back up.
2. Copy all files from `packages/` except your own `hbc_config.yaml` (new: `hbc_gridclock.yaml`, `hbc_price_guard.yaml`) and the folder
   `www/hbc-plus/` into your HA `www/` folder (the dashboard logo). Check the configuration and restart Home Assistant.
3. In Node-RED, delete every tab whose name starts with *HBC+*, import `node-red/hbc-plus-all-flows.json` and deploy.
4. Paste `dashboards/hbc_plus.yaml` again in the raw configuration editor.
5. On the Prices view choose *Grid Clock* and your supplier.
