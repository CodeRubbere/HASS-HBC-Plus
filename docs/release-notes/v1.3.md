# HBC+ v1.3 — know what your batteries earn

v1.3 shows what the batteries save you, keeps them calibrated, checks your setup with one button and tells you
when a newer release is out or a part of your installation is of another version. With default settings the
control works as in v1.2; only the battery calibration is on by default (*Auto*).

## What's new

- **Savings** — a new *Savings* view: what your energy cost with the batteries compared with without them, per
  day, month and year, split into stored PV and charging from the grid.
- **Measured round-trip efficiency** — measured over 7 days, with a suggested *Minimum spread* you take over with
  one button. Optionally Dynamic pairs on what a kWh really earns after losses (off by default).
- **Battery calibration** — a battery that has not been full for 14 days is charged to 100 % once, in the cheapest
  block of the night; its charge cutoff goes back afterwards. When tomorrow's sun will fill it anyway, nothing is planned.
- **Setup self-test** — one button on the *Batteries* view checks P1, battery entities, master mode, heartbeat,
  Node-RED, price sensor and notify targets, and measures on which phase each battery is connected. A different
  phase is only applied when you press *Apply phase map*.
- **Load forecast** — HBC+ learns how much the house uses per quarter (working days and weekend days apart).
  Optionally Dynamic charges only what the expensive quarters need (off by default).
- **One notification setting** — set *Notify targets* once on the *Settings* view, with a test button. Before, you
  set it in six places.
- **Strategy requests from Home Assistant** — automations can hold a strategy through the Strategy arbiter, with a
  time-out, without Node-RED (`script.hbc_strategy_hold`, also over MQTT). Active holds show on the Overview.
- **Power setpoint for Charge and Sell** — a flow such as HELIOS can run Charge or Sell at a planned power instead
  of the maximum, within the phase rules and the grid limit.
- **Batterijplan and HELIOS on the dashboard** — when Batterijplan (Live) or HELIOS steers, banners say so and the
  price chart shows their plan; the Dynamic modes are read-only while Batterijplan steers.
- **Update and version check** — the Overview says *HBC+ vX.Y.Z is available* when GitHub has a newer release, and
  which part (a package file, the flows or the dashboard) is of another version. A *Versions* table on the
  *Settings* view lists every part. Only the request itself goes to GitHub: no account, token or personal data.
- **Dashboard** — an *Executing now* card and a legend on the price chart, *Buy price* and *Sell price* tiles, and a
  clearer alert when the P1 meter stops sending values or HBC+ keeps logging errors (with a *Fix* line).
- **Fixes** — a lost connection to Home Assistant gives one warning instead of a stream of errors; the *PID
  analysis* graph no longer fails when a device clock runs ahead; every tariff source named *Grid Clock …* reads the
  Grid Clock feed.
- **Release zip** — from v1.3 a release has `hbc-plus-vX.Y.Z.zip` under *Assets*, with only what you install.
- Includes the fixes of v1.2.1 and v1.2.2.

## Updating from v1.2

1. Back up.
2. Copy all files from `packages/` except your own `hbc_config.yaml`. New: `hbc_notify.yaml` (**required**: without it
   HBC+ sends no notifications), `hbc_calibration.yaml`, `hbc_savings.yaml`, `hbc_selftest.yaml`,
   `hbc_load_forecast.yaml` and `hbc_batterijplan.yaml`. Check the configuration and restart Home Assistant.
3. In Node-RED, delete every tab whose name starts with *HBC+*, import `node-red/hbc-plus-all-flows.json` and deploy.
   New tab: *HBC+ Self-test v1.3*.
4. Paste `dashboards/hbc_plus.yaml` again in the raw configuration editor.
5. Had you set your own notify action? Put it in *Notify targets* on the *Settings* view and press *Send a test
   notification*.
6. Run the self-test once: *Batteries* → *Battery phase test* → *Run self-test*.
